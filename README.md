# Day14_Food_Delivary_Visualization
# Food Delivery Business Performance – Data Visualization Portfolio

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes a Food Delivery Business Performance dataset using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. The objective is to explore the data through different visualization techniques and identify meaningful patterns in food delivery business performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

* Changes in orders and revenue over time
* Revenue performance across different cities
* Order patterns across different cuisines
* Relationship between marketing spend and revenue
* Relationship between delivery time and customer ratings
* Impact of weather conditions on delivery performance
* Differences in customer ratings across order channels
* Relationships between numerical variables

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## 📈 Visualizations Included

The notebook contains:

* Line plots
* Bar charts
* Scatter plots
* Histograms
* Box plots
* Violin plots
* Count plots
* Correlation heatmap

Each visualization includes appropriate titles, axis labels, formatting, and a brief interpretation of the findings.

## 🔍 Key Findings

* Orders and revenue show variations over time.
* Revenue performance differs across cities.
* Customer demand varies across different cuisines.
* Marketing spend has a **weak positive relationship** with revenue, with a correlation of approximately **0.198**.
* Delivery time has a **strong negative relationship** with customer ratings, with a correlation of approximately **-0.876**.
* Weather conditions show differences in delivery times.
* Customer rating distributions vary across different order channels.
* The correlation heatmap provides an overall view of relationships between numerical variables.

## 📁 Project Files

* `Food_Delivery_Visualization_Portfolio.ipynb` – Complete visualization analysis with code, charts, interpretations, and conclusions.
* `Day14_Food_Delivery_Visualization_Dataset.csv` – Dataset used for the analysis.

## 📝 Conclusion

The analysis indicates that delivery efficiency is strongly associated with customer satisfaction, while marketing spend has a comparatively weak relationship with revenue. Differences in cities, cuisines, weather conditions, and order channels also provide useful insights into food delivery business performance.
